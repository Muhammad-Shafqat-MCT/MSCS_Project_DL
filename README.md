# Waste Type Object Detection & Tracking
Manual waste sorting is a significant challenge in modern waste management systems. Traditional sorting processes depend heavily on human workers, making them time-consuming, labor-intensive, and prone to mistakes. As the volume of waste generated by households, industries, and commercial sectors continues to increase, manual sorting becomes less efficient and more expensive. Human operators may misclassify waste materials due to fatigue, lack of consistency, or the complexity of mixed waste streams, leading to reduced recycling efficiency and higher operational costs.

Improper waste disposal is another major environmental issue. When recyclable materials are not correctly identified and separated, they often end up in landfills, causing unnecessary resource loss and environmental damage. Plastic waste can persist in the environment for hundreds of years, while improperly discarded metal, glass, and paper contribute to pollution and waste accumulation. Organic waste can also produce harmful greenhouse gases when not managed appropriately. These challenges highlight the need for intelligent and automated waste management solutions.

Advances in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Deep Learning, and Computer Vision (CV) have created new opportunities for automating waste sorting processes. Computer Vision systems can analyze images and video streams to identify and classify waste materials accurately and efficiently. Such systems reduce human intervention, improve sorting speed, and ensure consistent performance across large-scale waste processing facilities.

The primary goal of this project is to develop an automated Computer Vision-based waste detection system capable of recognizing five common waste categories: plastic, paper, metal, glass, and organic waste. Using the YOLO11 object detection model, the system can perform real-time detection and classification of waste items from images or live video feeds. Once identified, waste materials can be directed toward appropriate recycling or disposal channels.

By automating waste classification, the proposed system aims to increase recycling efficiency, reduce environmental pollution, lower operational costs, and support sustainable waste management practices. The solution can be deployed in recycling plants, smart bins, and urban waste management systems, contributing to cleaner communities and a more environmentally responsible future.
